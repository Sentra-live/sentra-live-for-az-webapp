Structural Health Monitoring Digital Twin

A 3D model shows what a structure looks like. BIM describes what it is made of. A digital twin tracks what it is doing. Here is the difference, the architecture that joins them, and where our twins sit.

Three Terms, Three Things

3D Model ≠ BIM ≠ Digital Twin

The difference decides what the deliverable can do for you.

3D model

Geometry and appearance, from drawings, scans or photogrammetry.

  • AnswersWhat does it look like?
  • UnitSurfaces, points
  • After handoverDoes not change

BIM

Geometry plus objects, properties and relationships, usually authored at design stage.

  • AnswersWhat is it made of, and where?
  • UnitBuilding or structural elements
  • After handoverOnly if re-modelled

Digital twin

The asset as it is now and how it got there: components with an identity, a condition and a history.

  • AnswersWhat state is this component in, and what happened to it?
  • UnitComponents with IDs
  • After handoverKeeps accepting inspections, maintenance and live readings

Our working definition: a component-level model of the asset as built, plus its inspection and maintenance history, plus live measurements bound to those components, plus the analysis that turns measurements into a condition.

The formula 3D/BIM model + live IoT data + analytics + lifecycle information = operational digital twin describes L3 and L4. L3 is delivered; L4 is the next step.

Binding Sensors to Components

Every Channel Knows Its Member

Select a component of an open web girder span to see its asset ID and what is measured on it. Layout shown on the geometry of Bridge No. 60 DN.

BR-60DN/SPAN1/GIRDER-US/BF-MID

Main girder bottom chord

Measured
Strain, stress range, fatigue cycles
Sensors
Vibrating wire strain gauges
Why it matters
Stress range spectra feed the member’s fatigue assessment directly.
BR-60DN/SPAN1/CROSS-GIRDER-05

Cross girder

Measured
Strain, load transfer, secondary stress
Sensors
Strain gauges near bearings
Why it matters
Shows how load passes from deck to main girders.
BR-60DN/SPAN1/DECK

Deck and track

Measured
Acceleration, vibration amplitude and frequency
Sensors
Accelerometers
Why it matters
Natural frequency and damping tracked against temperature for stiffness change.
BR-60DN/SPAN1/BEARING-A1

Bearing A1

Measured
Displacement, rotation, temperature
Sensors
LVDT / laser distance, tiltmeter, temperature
Why it matters
Separates thermal movement from a seized or damaged bearing.
BR-60DN/ABUT-A

Abutment A

Measured
Tilt, settlement
Sensors
Tiltmeter, GNSS
Why it matters
Rotation of the substructure is the earliest sign of settlement or scour.
BR-60DN/SPAN1/TOP-CHORD

Top chord and diagonals

Measured
Geometry, condition records
Sensors
Reality capture (drone + TLS); strain where fatigue-critical
Why it matters
Inspection findings and defect notes attach to the member they describe.
