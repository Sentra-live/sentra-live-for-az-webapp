Ports & Marine
Monitoring

Corrosion tracking, jetty tilt and structural load monitoring for quays, wharves and offshore platforms in harsh marine environments.

Talk to an Expert Explore Solution

Why Port
Monitoring Matters

Ports and marine structures operate in one of the most corrosive environments on earth. Constant exposure to saltwater, wave action, and heavy cargo loads accelerates structural degradation and reduces asset lifespan.

Sentra's IoT monitoring solutions provide continuous insight into the health of jetties, wharves, quay walls, and offshore platforms — tracking corrosion rates, structural tilt, and load distribution to enable data-driven maintenance decisions.

Key Monitoring Parameters

  • Corrosion rate and environmental monitoring
  • Jetty and wharf tilt and inclination
  • Structural load and mooring force measurement
  • Concrete crack monitoring in marine structures
  • Wave-induced vibration analysis
  • Scour and foundation settlement tracking

Relevant Solutions

Sentra's solutions for port and marine structure monitoring.

Asset Monitoring & Management Lifecycle management for port infrastructure and marine assets. Structural Health Monitoring Continuous structural assessment for marine infrastructure. Geotechnical & Foundation Monitoring Scour and foundation monitoring for marine structures.

Recommended Hardware

Sensors and edge hardware commonly deployed for this application.

Strain Gauge Structural load and stress tracking on quay walls, jetties and marine steelwork. GNSS Meter Berth and structure displacement monitoring for port infrastructure. Accelerometer Vibration monitoring for marine structures under vessel impact and wave loading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Corrosion monitoring tracks the rate of material degradation on steel and reinforced concrete marine structures, using dedicated sensors alongside strain and load data, so asset owners can plan maintenance before section loss compromises structural capacity.

Strain gauges mounted on quay walls, jetties and berthing structures continuously measure stress from vessel impact, wave action and operational loading, streaming data through corrosion-resistant enclosures suited to the marine environment.

Our sensor enclosures are IP68-rated for full submersion and wide temperature ranges, and installations for coastal and marine sites use corrosion-resistant mounting hardware suited to salt-air exposure.
