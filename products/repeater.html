Overview

Extend Sensor Connectivity for Continuous Monitoring

The K20 Edge Repeater strengthens wireless sensor networks by boosting signal reach and maintaining continuous communication between sensors and gateways. It retransmits data from associated nodes to a main gateway using a tree network topology, allowing data to travel across multiple repeaters (up to 8 hops) to extend coverage.

Ideal for challenging terrains, tunnels, mines and extended infrastructure projects — ensuring critical tilt, vibration and GNSS data is transmitted reliably even where direct gateway signals cannot reach.

Core Capabilities

What Makes the K20 Edge Repeater Different

Field-proven range extension technology designed for underground and challenging monitoring environments.

Multi-Hop Range Extension

Tree network topology supports up to 8 hops with 150m to 3km per hop — extending coverage into tunnels, mines and dense urban environments where direct signals cannot reach.

High Transmission Reliability

>99.7% transmission success rate in default network configuration (3.125 messages/min). High demand mode supports 8 messages/min with >98.7% success rate.

Seamless Network Integration

Operates as a transparent range extender within existing Worldsensing networks. Compatible with both Cloud and Edge gateway deployments without additional configuration.

IP67 Rugged Design

Weatherproof IP67-rated enclosure protects against dust, water immersion and extreme temperatures. Operating range from −40°C to +60°C for the harshest conditions.

Simple PoE Power

Power over Ethernet (PoE) supports both mode A and mode B — a single cable delivers power and data. USB-C also available for local access. Mean consumption just 4.5W.

Multi-Band Support

Available in standard (863-928MHz) and 915R (902-928MHz) variants to match regional regulatory requirements across EMEA, Americas, India and APAC.

Applications

Where the K20 Edge Repeater Deploys

Deploy the K20 Edge Repeater wherever wireless sensor networks need to reach deeper or farther.

Underground Mining

Extend network coverage deep into underground mine tunnels where direct gateway signals cannot penetrate. Maintain full sensor visibility for slope stability and seismic monitoring.

Metro & Rail Tunnels

Continuous sensor data from tunnel linings, track geometry and ventilation systems across long underground corridors with multi-hop repeater chains.

Large Bridge Networks

Bridge sensors located far from the gateway location can connect through repeaters — ensuring complete coverage across long-span and multi-structure sites.

Remote Terrain

Deploy sensor networks across valleys, hillsides and remote slopes where line-of-sight to the gateway is obstructed by terrain features.

Urban Canyons

Overcome signal blockage from dense buildings and infrastructure in urban monitoring environments where direct LoRaWAN range is limited.

Large Construction Sites

Temporary repeater deployment for construction monitoring networks covering expansive excavation and building sites with multiple sensor types.
