Overview

Benefits Built for Accuracy

From bridge decks under live traffic loads to industrial machinery experiencing high-frequency vibration — Sentra wired sensors deliver the data precision engineers rely on for confident structural health decisions.

High-Precision Detection

Capture minute vibrations and micro-strain with exceptional accuracy across a wide dynamic range — delivering comprehensive structural health assessment data.

Rugged & Reliable

IP67-rated weatherproof enclosures survive full immersion, extreme temperatures and harsh site conditions without signal degradation.

Predictive Analytics

AI-powered cloud analytics identify patterns and trends, enabling proactive maintenance before failures occur — reducing lifecycle costs significantly.

Easy Integration

Seamless connectivity with LoRaWAN, 4G LTE, RS-485 and Bluetooth. Integrates with existing SCADA, BIM and cloud platforms without reconfiguration.

Core Capabilities

What Makes Our Wired Sensors Different

Enterprise-grade hardware and software designed for mission-critical structural monitoring applications.

3-Axis MEMS Accelerometer

Captures acceleration across all three axes simultaneously with a ±16g range — ideal for vibration, seismic response and modal analysis on any structure type.

Strain Gauge Technology

Spot-weldable and 4100-series strain gauges for direct surface bonding on steel and concrete — measuring micro-strain under static and dynamic loading conditions.

Multi-Protocol Connectivity

Supports LoRaWAN for long-range wireless, 4G LTE for cellular backhaul, Bluetooth for local configuration and RS-485 for wired SCADA integration.

Cloud Analytics Platform

Real-time dashboards, historical trend analysis and automated threshold alerts accessible via web or mobile. BIM and GIS integration ready out of the box.

Real-Time Alerts

Configurable threshold-based alerts via SMS, email and push notifications — instant notification when sensor readings exceed safe operating parameters.

Long Battery Life

Up to 10 years on replaceable battery cells. AC/DC power input and solar panel options available for permanent installations requiring zero maintenance.

Applications

Where Our Wired Sensors Deploy

Sentra wired sensors are designed for demanding monitoring applications where signal quality, accuracy and long-term stability are non-negotiable.

Bridge Monitoring

Traffic-induced vibration, dynamic load testing and fatigue monitoring on road and rail bridges for continuous structural health assessment.

Buildings & High-Rise

Seismic response monitoring, wind-induced sway and floor vibration measurement in high-rise structures during construction and service life.

Tunnels & Metro

Convergence and vibration monitoring in tunnelling operations — real-time data from excavation face through to final lining installation.

Industrial Machinery

High-frequency vibration monitoring on rotating equipment, compressors, pumps and turbines for predictive maintenance and fault detection.

Wind Turbines

Tower vibration, blade deflection and foundation monitoring on onshore and offshore wind installations throughout their operational lifecycle.

Dam & Hydraulic Structures

Seismic event detection and dynamic response monitoring on large dams — complementing static instrumentation with vibration data.
