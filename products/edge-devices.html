Core Capabilities

What Makes Our Edge Devices Different

Purpose-built for unattended infrastructure monitoring with a hardware-software stack designed for reliability at the edge.

Superior Wireless Connectivity

LoRaWAN long-range communications up to 15 km with support for 4G LTE, Bluetooth and RS-485 fallback. Operates on dedicated networks without internet dependency.

Durable, IP68-Rated Hardware

Industrial-grade enclosures rated IP68 for full submersion resistance. Operates reliably from −40°C to +85°C — from Arctic cold to desert heat.

Ultra-Long Battery Life

High-energy C and D size battery cells with up to 25 years of field life. User-replaceable without special tools. Solar panel integration available for remote sites.

Mobile App Configuration

Configure devices in the field via the Sentra mobile app. Built-in setup wizard, radio signal coverage testing, and data sampling — no laptop needed.

Cloud & SCADA Integration

Transmits data securely to cloud platforms for real-time dashboards, historical analysis, and automated alerting. BIM and GIS integration ready.

Configurable Reporting Intervals

Set reporting periods from 30 seconds to 24 hours based on project needs. Triggered event detection for seismic, tilt or vibration anomalies.

Applications

Where Our Edge Devices Deploy

Sentra edge devices are designed for large-scale, unattended monitoring across surface and underground environments.

Bridge Monitoring

Continuous vibration, tilt and deflection monitoring on road and rail bridges for structural health assessment.

Building & High-Rise

Track settlement, tilt and seismic response in real time during construction and throughout service life.

Dam & Reservoir

Automated piezometer, tiltmeter and GNSS networks for early warning and seepage detection on embankment dams.

Tunnels & Metro

Underground sensor networks for convergence, vibration and groundwater monitoring in tunnelling projects.

Geotechnical Slopes

Landslide and slope stability monitoring with inclinometers, tiltmeters and rainfall sensors on remote terrain.

Industrial Infrastructure

Pump houses, tanks, silos and industrial facilities where predictive maintenance and vibration data drive uptime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions About Our Edge Devices?

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team — we're happy to help.

Sentra edge devices are suitable for bridges, buildings, tunnels, dams, industrial facilities, wind turbines, slopes, retaining walls and any structure where continuous vibration, tilt or deformation monitoring is critical for safety and performance.

Devices use high-energy C or D cell batteries with up to 25 years of field life depending on reporting interval. Batteries are user-replaceable without tools. AC/DC power input and solar panel integration options are also available for continuous or solar-powered deployments.

Our devices support LoRaWAN for long-range wireless communication (up to 15 km), 4G LTE for cellular connectivity, Bluetooth for local mobile app configuration, and RS-485 for wired integration with existing SCADA or data acquisition systems.

Yes. All devices are rated IP68 for full submersion protection and carry industrial-grade certification. Operating temperature range is −40°C to +85°C, making them suitable for underground mines, coastal bridges, mountain slopes and tropical environments.

Data is transmitted to Sentra's cloud platform and accessible via web dashboards or mobile apps. The platform includes real-time visualisation, historical trend analysis, automated threshold alerts, and export integrations for BIM and GIS platforms.

A single Sentra LoRaWAN gateway network can support 1000+ wireless sensors and data loggers simultaneously — ideal for large-scale infrastructure projects like dam instrumentation networks or city-wide bridge monitoring programmes.
