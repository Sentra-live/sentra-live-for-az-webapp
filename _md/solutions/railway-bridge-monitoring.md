## Why Railway Bridges Need Measured Data

Railway bridge monitoring under live train loads: strain, deflection, vibration and tilt sensors, load testing and residual fatigue life assessment to RDSO and BS-106R guidance.

### Railway Bridge Monitoring

Faster trains and heavier axle loads are running over railway bridges, many of them decades old. These bridges have to be checked against how they are operated today, not only against the loads they were designed for.

Fatigue damage accumulates through load cycles, not through time. Every train that crosses puts members through a stress range, so a member can look untouched and still be well through its fatigue life. The only way to know is to measure how the bridge responds when a train crosses.

## How a Railway Bridge Monitoring Programme Runs

From the first survey to a residual fatigue life figure, based on our railway bridge instrumentation and load testing work.

### How it is deployed

We survey geometry, camber and bearing condition before instrumenting. Controlled train runs set reference stress and deflection profiles. Then we record continuously at operational speeds, log timestamped data automatically to the cloud, and validate it against the design model.

### Load testing

Under RDSO guidance the test conditions include a stationary locomotive and wagon, slow and design-speed moving loads, accelerating loads, and braking at about 20 km/h. Which tests run, and in what order, is agreed with the railway beforehand.

### Monitoring period

Our steel plate girder deployment logged continuously for three months at 100 Hz. For fatigue assessment, the methodology recommends around four weeks at 50–100 Hz, plus 72 hours of normal traffic for a selected train.

### What drives the cost

There is no fixed price; scope is set by the structure and the programme. The main drivers are:

- Number of spans and members instrumented

- Whether load testing and FE model calibration are included

- Track access and block arrangements

- Sampling rate and monitoring duration

- Depth of fatigue assessment and reporting

### Alerts and dashboards

Engineers watch bridge behaviour in real time and set alarm thresholds. The system automatically flags deviations beyond permissible values, and multi-tier alerts reach your team by SMS, email and dashboard.

### What you get at the end

Measured stress range, deflection, natural frequency and fatigue damage index, each against its limit. You also get a calibrated finite element model and a residual fatigue life estimate.
