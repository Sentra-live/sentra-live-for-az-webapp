### Predictive Maintenance and Condition Monitoring

Preventive maintenance services equipment at fixed intervals, whatever its actual condition. Condition monitoring gives continuous visibility of each asset's health, so maintenance is triggered by real deterioration.

That avoids replacing healthy components too early, and catches deteriorating ones before they fail. The result costs less and is more reliable than either time-based or reactive maintenance.

## Faults Vibration Reveals Early

Each fault has a characteristic vibration signature, visible well before the overall level moves.

Imbalance Dominant 1× peak, radial, growing with the square of speed

Misalignment Strong 2× content, often with significant axial vibration

Looseness A series of 1×, 2×, 3× harmonics, sometimes half-order

Bearing defects Non-synchronous frequencies set by bearing geometry

Gear damage Gear mesh frequency with sidebands at shaft speed

Cavitation Broadband high-frequency noise in pumps

## Rolling Out Condition Monitoring in a Working Plant

What maintenance and reliability teams should plan for, from the first criticality ranking to predictive alerts.

### How it is deployed

We catalogue your assets and rank them by criticality, then put wireless sensors on the critical ones. After data integration we set baselines and alert thresholds. Then we run alert management and lifecycle reporting, including remaining useful life projections.

### Sampling that catches faults early

A sensor that reports only an RMS value every few hours catches a machine that is already deteriorating badly. Early warning needs devices that capture a full waveform and transmit a spectrum.

### A network built for the plant

Steel structure, concrete and rotating machinery make a hostile radio environment. LoRaWAN gateways handle range and penetration well for periodic spectra, and repeaters cover the awkward corners.

### What drives the cost

There is no fixed price; scope is set by the structure and the programme. The main drivers are:

- Number of assets and sensors per asset

- Sampling each failure mode needs

- Gateways, repeaters and edge processing

- Integration with CMMS, SCADA or BMS

### How long it takes

20–50 critical assets typically go from site survey to live dashboard in 4–8 weeks. Alerts are threshold-based during the first 4–8 week baseline period, and predictive alerts improve as the models learn each asset.

### Alerts and integration

Alerts arrive by dashboard, SMS and email, with diagnostic context, and data exports as CSV. If alerts or readings need to reach a specific CMMS or ERP, we confirm the route before it goes into a proposal.
