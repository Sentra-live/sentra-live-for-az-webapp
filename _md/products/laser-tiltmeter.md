Overview

## Non-Intrusive Convergence Monitoring with Laser Precision

The Laser Tiltmeter combines a visible laser distance meter (class II, 650 nm) with a 3-axis MEMS accelerometer in a single rugged enclosure. It provides non-intrusive, remote monitoring of inclinations, movements, and differential settlements of slopes or infrastructure — ideal for tunneling projects where it can be relocated after stabilization.

The device stores up to 100,000 readings including time, distance, and 3-axis tilt measurements. Data is transmitted via LoRa radio communications to the CMT platform for real-time visualization and analysis.

Core Capabilities

## What Makes the Laser Tiltmeter Different

Purpose-built for non-intrusive convergence monitoring with dual-sensor fusion and long-range wireless connectivity.

#### Laser Precision

Class II visible laser (650 nm) with 0.1 mm resolution. Accuracy of ±1 mm at 10 m, ±4 mm at 50 m, and ±8 mm at 150 m distance. Repeatability of 0.15 mm for consistent measurements.

#### Extended Battery Life

Powered by 2 × 3.6V C-size replaceable batteries. Up to 14.3 years of unattended operation at 6-hour reporting interval (20 m distance). User-replaceable without special tools.

#### IP68 Rated & Rugged

Industrial-grade IP68 enclosure for harsh environments. Operating range from −20°C to +60°C — suitable for tunnel and outdoor deployments.

#### Dual-Sensor Fusion

Combines laser distance measurement with 3-axis tilt sensing (MEMS accelerometer). Correlates distance and angle data for precise convergence and settlement analysis.

#### Cloud & Edge Management

Configure locally via CMT Edge for single-network deployments, or connect to CMT Cloud for multi-project, multi-network oversight from anywhere.

#### Mobile App Configuration

Quick setup wizard via Worldsensing App with Bluetooth connectivity. USB-C connector also available for direct field configuration.

Explore the range of monitoring solutions enabled by the Laser Tiltmeter across tunneling, structural, and geotechnical applications.

### Tunneling

##### Convergence Monitoring

Measure tunnel wall convergence and crown settlement during excavation. The device can be relocated after stabilization, providing cost-effective monitoring through construction phases.

##### Shotcrete Lining

Monitor shotcrete lining deformation during NATM tunneling. Track radial and tangential displacement with laser precision for safety assurance.

### Structural & Geotechnical

##### Crack & Joint Monitoring

Monitor crack width changes and joint movements across structural elements. Laser provides non-contact measurement for hard-to-reach locations.

##### Slope Displacement

Track surface displacement on slopes and embankments with sub-millimeter accuracy for early warning of potential failure.

##### Settlement Monitoring

Differential settlement measurement across structures and foundations using laser distance triangulation.

##### Retaining Wall Deflection

Non-contact monitoring of retaining wall deflection and rotation during and after construction.
