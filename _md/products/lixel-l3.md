Overview

## A New Benchmark for Spatial Capture

The Lixel L3 is available in 16-channel (320,000 pts/s) and 32-channel (640,000 pts/s) LiDAR configurations, both covering 0.7m to 120m. Independent validation confirms 5mm relative accuracy and roughly 2cm absolute accuracy (RMSE), with levelness as tight as 0.005° when fused with RTK. A single scan yields three complementary deliverables: a true-color point cloud, a textured mesh, and a photorealistic Lixel CyberColor 3D Gaussian Splat.

Three 50MP cameras on a 1/1.3-inch sensor capture 4K HDR true-color imagery, while an integrated SLAM system: real-time vision-aided localization, loop-closure optimisation and a high-accuracy IMU: keeps the scan stable through large, complex sites. A built-in RTK module and 4.3-inch, 1000-nit display let surveyors monitor coverage live in the field, and typically cover around 100 square metres of high-accuracy data in 10 minutes.
