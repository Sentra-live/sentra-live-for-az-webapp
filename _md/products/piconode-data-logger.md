Overview

## Compact Wireless Data Acquisition for Tight Spaces

The Piconode is an ultra-compact, 3-channel wireless data logger designed for long-term, unattended monitoring in confined spaces. It includes one configurable analog channel (voltage, potentiometer, Full Wheatstone bridge), one precision thermistor channel, and one pulse counter channel — all in a fully potted, IP68-rated enclosure.

With its compact form factor and long-range LoRa communications, the Piconode is ideal for high-density monitoring applications where space is at a premium — such as manholes, inside structural joints, boreholes, and cramped construction sites.

Purpose-built for high-density, space-constrained monitoring with versatile sensor inputs and extreme reliability.

LoRaWAN communications reaching up to 4 km in urban environments. Reliable data transmission even from manholes, tunnels, and below-grade installations.

Applications

## Monitoring Solutions for Every Environment

Explore the wide range of monitoring solutions the Piconode enables across geotechnical, structural, environmental and remote process monitoring.

### Geotechnical Monitoring

##### Pore Water Pressure & Level

Connect piezometers and water level sensors to the configurable analog channel for continuous groundwater monitoring in dams, slopes and excavations.

##### Cross-Crack Movements

Wireline extensometers, crackmeters and displacement sensors for monitoring tension cracks across landslides and slopes using the analog or pulse input.

##### Load in Rock Bolts & Ground Anchors

Load cells connected via Full Wheatstone bridge for monitoring anchor systems throughout their lifecycle — from load tests to long-term service.

##### In-ground Displacement

Multi-point borehole extensometers and displacement sensors for monitoring ground movement in boreholes and excavations.

### Structural & Environmental Monitoring

##### Movements Across Surface Cracks

Crackmeters and draw wire sensors connected to the configurable channel for monitoring surface crack movement in structures.

##### Temperature Monitoring

Precision thermistor channel for monitoring temperature in concrete, soil and structures. Internal temperature sensor logs with each reading.

##### Rainfall & Precipitation

Connect tipping bucket rain gauges to the pulse counter channel for remote rainfall monitoring in environmental and hydrological applications.

##### Open Channel Flow & Pond Level

Level sensors and flow meters connected to the configurable analog or pulse counter channels for monitoring water flow and pond levels.

### Remote Process Monitoring

##### Pressure & Strain Monitoring

Strain gauges and pressure sensors connected via Full Wheatstone bridge for monitoring structural stress and pressure in industrial installations.

##### Pulse Counting & Flow Totalization

Water meters, flow totalizers, and pulse-output sensors connected to the dedicated pulse counter channel for remote consumption monitoring.

##### Soil Water Tension & Moisture

Tensiometers and soil moisture sensors for assessing water tension in geotechnical, agricultural and environmental applications.

##### Dewatering Operations

Monitor water flow and pump activity with flow meters connected to the pulse counter channel for remote dewatering management.

##

The Piconode is an ultra-compact wireless data logger designed for long-term, unattended monitoring in confined spaces. It captures data from voltage, potentiometer, Wheatstone bridge, thermistor, and pulse-output sensors — making it ideal for environmental, geotechnical, and structural monitoring where space is at a premium.

##

The Piconode supports voltage (±7.5 mV/V for FWB, 0–5 VDC for single-ended), potentiometer (0–5 VDC), Full Wheatstone bridge, thermistor (0 Ω to 2 MΩ), and pulse counter (0 to 4.3 billion pulses) inputs. This covers load cells, strain gauges, pressure sensors, NTC thermistors, rain gauges, flow meters, displacement sensors, and more.

##

Battery life depends on the selected reporting period and battery configuration. With a 1-hour reporting interval, the 1-cell configuration achieves 8.6 years, while the 2-cell configuration reaches 17.2 years. With a 6-hour interval, the 2-cell configuration exceeds 25 years. Batteries are user-replaceable.

In open sight, the range can reach up to 15 km (9.3 mi). In dense urban environments, up to 4 km (2.5 mi). When placed in a manhole, the range extends up to 2 km (1.2 mi), and in tunnels it reaches up to 4 km (2.5 mi). Range depends on terrain, environment and antenna configuration.

##

The Piconode can be configured wirelessly via the Worldsensing App using Bluetooth — no laptop needed. The app provides a wiring assistant, field sampling mode, signal coverage testing, and full diagnostics. For remote management, configuration can be done through CMT Edge or CMT Cloud platforms.
