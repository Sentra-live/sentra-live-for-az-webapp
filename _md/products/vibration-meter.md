Operational Modes

## Two Powerful Analysis Modes

Configurable operational modes with advanced algorithms for various vibration standards and assessment requirements. Available with internal antenna for compact deployment.

##### PPV-Based Mode

Reports Peak Particle Velocity (PPVx,y,z in mm/s), frequency (fx,y,z in Hz), temperature (ºC), and Peak Vector Sum (PVS). Velocity range: 0-60 mm/s (±0.01 mm/s). Frequency output range: 1-100 Hz (±0.5 Hz). Ideal for blast vibration monitoring and structural response assessment.

##### MTVV-Based Mode

Reports maximum transient vibration value (LAW-x,y,z in dBm) and temperature (ºC). Acceleration range: ±2g. Frequency weighting: Wm. Designed for human comfort assessment and continuous vibration monitoring per ISO standards.

Alert mode with secondary threshold enables automatic reporting of critical events via real-time notifications.
